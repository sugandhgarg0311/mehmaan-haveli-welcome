# Mehmaan — Complete Website Design & UX Direction




Act as a senior product designer, creative director and senior frontend engineer.




Your task is to redesign the entire Mehmaan website into a premium, vintage Indian heritage-inspired hospitality platform.




Do not create a generic SaaS website, generic travel booking platform or modern startup landing page.




The website should feel like entering a beautiful Indian haveli, exploring Indian traditions, discovering local experiences and being welcomed into someone's home.




The design must combine traditional Indian visual identity with modern, intuitive and frictionless UX.




## 1. Core Brand Identity




Brand: Mehmaan




Core philosophy: अतिथि देवो भव — The guest is God.




Welcome expression:

"आइए, पधारिए"

"Aaiye, padhariye — Come in, you are welcome."




Brand positioning:




Millions of Indian families preserve beautiful traditions, food, art, crafts and experiences. Mehmaan helps them share their culture and hospitality with travellers.




The website must communicate:

- Indian hospitality.

- Cultural richness.

- Warmth and belonging.

- Authentic local experiences.

- Trust and transparency.

- Traditional Indian craftsmanship.




Important: Atithi Devo Bhava represents hospitality shared across Indian families and communities. Do not portray it as a religious ritual. Never ask hosts to perform rituals for guests.PAGE BACKGROUND

Feeling: warm, aged, handmade

Reference: cream parchment with soft texture

Material: parchment texture (Unsplash), very subtle, colour #F3E6CC

Rule: text must stay readable; texture opacity low




PAGE FRAME AND SECTION DIVIDERS

Feeling: festive, textile, crafted

Reference: red/yellow block-print border with triangles and flowers

Material: one horizontal border strip image, repeated with CSS border-image

Rule: max 32 px tall; only at the top of the page and between sections




HERO

Feeling: royal, welcoming, painted

Reference: Pichwai lotus painting inside a scalloped arch on maroon

Material: a CC0 museum painting inside the scalloped arch; maroon background

          (#5C1A1B) with mandala corners in gold; heading "आइए, पधारिए" in Rozha One, gold

Rule: one painting only; the button stays clearly visible.
Next.js or Vite + Tailwind + Motion for UI + GSAP/ScrollTrigger + Lenis for scroll storytelling + R3F + Drei for 3D. I want a traditonal indian door that opens with a sdoor openign sound and that opens to our website/“Photorealistic straight-on view of a traditional Indian double door, golden carved wood with brass floral rosettes and ring knockers, set inside a pink sandstone scalloped Mughal arch with marigold toran, soft warm light, symmetrical, closed doors, no people, no text”
